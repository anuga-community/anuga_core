\section{EA Test 2: filling of floodplain depressions}

Test~2 of the Environment Agency benchmark \cite{NeelzPender2013} asks
for the inundation extent and final depths after low-momentum flow over a
complex topography. A 2000~m $\times$ 2000~m square holds a $4 \times 4$
grid of identical depressions about 0.5~m deep, on an average slope of
1:1500 to the south and 1:3000 to the east
(Figure~\ref{fig:ea2_setup}). An inflow rising to 20~m$^3$/s, held until
86~min and stopped at 91~min (97,200~m$^3$ in all) enters along the
100~m of the western edge below the north-western corner. All edges are
closed and the bed starts dry. Manning's $n = 0.03$, the specified
resolution is 20~m (here triangles of at most 400~m$^2$, 15,366 in all),
and the run lasts 48~h. The depressions are numbered in columns from the
south-west corner.

In the report (Section~4.3.3) the full shallow-water packages agreed on
the outcome. A transient peak forms near the inflow, about 0.2~m above
the final level at point~4, 0.1~m at points 3 and 8 and 0.05~m at points
2 and 7. Depressions 1--4 and 6--8 overflow and drain back to the lowest
sill out of each; depressions 5, 10, 11 and 12 receive too little water to
fill; 13--16 stay dry. The final levels differed by a few centimetres at
most. The test separates the packages that carry momentum, whose flow
reaches depressions 10--12 to the east, from those that do not, whose
flow follows the steepest slope south and leaves them dry. \anuga{}
reproduces all of this. Its final levels in depressions 1--4 and 6--8
lie 4--12~mm above their sills (computed from the DEM), as they drain
slowly; at points 1--4, 7, 8, 11 and 12 they lie within 11~mm of the
range of TUFLOW's five current solvers \cite{BMT2017}. Point~5 ends
2~cm below TUFLOW's range, but it is still filling at 48~h, as it was
for every package. On a mesh four times finer (100~m$^2$, 61,601
triangles) the final levels move by at most 12~mm (at point~5) and by
under 2~mm elsewhere. The checks (Figure~\ref{fig:ea2_checks}) are the
volume balance, these outcomes and a stored baseline per flow algorithm.
